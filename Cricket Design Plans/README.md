### Routing and authority in one paragraph

A participant speaks to **their own Cricket**. Individual Crickets can communicate directly inside the Cricket Network when collaboration is relevant, without merging personal experiences or disclosing unrelated private information. A simple issue (for example, a muted microphone or menu question) stays with the personal Cricket. If a larger technical or production problem appears, Crickets share relevant factual observations, inform the participant and ask whether deeper investigation is wanted; an authorized request travels through **Jeremy Cricket to central Jeremy**, who can work with E-Pete, the physics engines and Pub Manager as necessary. The appropriately authorized person or service makes consequential decisions. Urgent safety/security events bypass ordinary Cricket triage. Jeremy Cricket is the **coordinator**, while central Jeremy retains the **older Jeremy duties**.
